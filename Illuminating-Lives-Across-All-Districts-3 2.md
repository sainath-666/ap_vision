## Andhra Pradesh Vision Outreach Program
# Illuminating Lives Across All Districts

A technology-driven, data-centric initiative to bring quality eye care and

affordable spectacles to every citizen across all 26 districts of Andhra Pradesh.


Akriti Ophthalmic Pvt Ltd


Hyderabad, TG, India


## The Unseen Challenge

Millions of citizens in Andhra Pradesh live with preventable or treatable vision

impairments 4 silently affecting their ability to learn, work, and thrive.












## Our Vision: A Comprehensive Outreach Program

#### Programme Goal

To conduct widespread vision screening

and distribute high-quality, affordable

eyeglasses to all citizens in need across

every district of Andhra Pradesh 4 leaving

no one behind.

#### Our Approach


A fully technology-driven, data-centric

model ensuring efficiency, transparency,

and end-to-end accountability 4 from first

screening to spectacle delivery and follow
up referral.
